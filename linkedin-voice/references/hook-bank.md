# Shaw's LinkedIn Hook Bank

A labeled library of proven hooks to **retrieve and remix** instead of writing from scratch. 50 hooks total: **35 from Shaw** (real posts, Jul 2025–Jul 2026, with metrics) + **15 borrowed** from top creators (patterns to adapt, not copy).

Metrics: `imp` = impressions (reach); `eng` = (reactions+comments+shares)/impressions. A hook is the **first two lines** — both sit above the "…see more" fold, and both must earn the expand (line 1 stops the scroll, line 2 re-hooks).

# How to use this bank

**Weak openers (avoid):** the lowest-pull hooks open on an abstract category trend with no person, number, or turn ("AI adoption is becoming table stakes…" stalled at 1,014 imp). Lead with a concrete subject in the first four words: **I, you, a number, or a named role** — never a concept.

**Reach vs engagement:** reach rewards broad/aspirational/curiosity hooks (receipts, "so back", salary-safety, milestones); engagement rewards opinion/identity hooks (#4, #6, #9, #13, norm-violation). Pick the lever before the mechanism.

**Structural constants across the winners:** two-line shape (claim → turn, whitespace between); ellipsis open loop; first four words carry it; colloquial > corporate.

**The borrowed half of a hook menu.** Every menu is 10 candidates: 5 in Shaw's voice, 5 in named creators' voices (see Hook process in `SKILL.md`). Pull the borrowed half from Part 2, and always name whose register each one is in — "more Matt Gray, less Hormozi" is a note Shaw can give in three words, but only if the attribution is on the page. State the tradeoff out loud too: these registers run guru-ward against Shaw's peer voice (Principle 3), so a borrowed hook buys punch and spends credibility. He can take that trade knowingly, and has, but it should be a choice rather than a drift.

**Rotation rule:** no mechanism repeats within ~3–4 weeks. The three NEW mechanisms (14–16) exist so there's always a fresh lane to rotate into.
