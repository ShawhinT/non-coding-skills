# Channel: One-Pagers

A single-page PDF or doc that sells an offer — sent to warm leads post-call, dropped in a DM, or linked from outreach. The compressed cousin of a landing page: same DNA, less real estate.

Lock before drafting: warm-post-call vs cold reader, the single CTA, the offer (container + outcome + timeframe + price), customer-language source.

## Channel moves

1. **One CTA.** Landing pages can sometimes afford two; one-pagers can't.
2. **Proof beats promises.** Warm readers already heard the pitch — weight the page toward named clients, specific numbers, screenshots, verbatim testimonials. Describing instead of showing wastes space.
3. **Lead with the unsignable angle.** The first block makes the reader think *"this is specifically for me."* If a competitor's name swaps in, start over.
4. **Cut the deadweight.** "About us" paragraphs, "why AI matters" preambles, undifferentiated process steps. Every bonus names the next-problem-after-buying it solves: "30 days of async access — because the setup will break in week 2" beats "Bonus: email support."
5. **One page means one page.** Running to two is a failed format — cut, don't shrink the font.
6. **Never bury the price.** Disclosed prominently or deliberately framed ("priced by scope — 15-min call").
7. **Post-call, personal beats formal.** A one-pager attached to a DM can open "Hey [name], here's what we talked about."

## Default skeleton

Title + subhead (outcome + container + timeframe, 2 lines) → who this is for (3–5 bullets) → the promise (one paragraph) → what you get → proof (1–2 named results + a verbatim quote) → investment + CTA. Full guarantee/process sections → `sales-letter-writer`.

## Worked example — title + subhead (competitor-led angle)

> *You don't need a $60k AI consulting engagement. You need 30 days and a plan.*
> *A 1:1 intensive where we build your first AI automations together — no dev team, no SaaS subscription you'll forget about.*

Angle choice tracks traffic: competitor-led hits hardest for warm leads who've been burned; outcome-led ("Reclaim 20–40 hours a month by Q3") works better cold.
