# Channel: Event Registration Pages (Luma)

Registration pages for free live events on Luma (AI Builder Academy calendar). The platform supplies the chrome (date, host, register button) — everything rides on four blocks. Work through them one at a time and lock each with Shaw; don't one-shot the page.

## The four blocks

1. **Title.** Series pattern: **"Claude [Code] for [Audience]: [Outcome]"** — signals who it's for in the first three words and keeps the catalog scannable. The outcome half passes the filter alone (a falsifiable number beats a theme), and the title verb can be more aggressive than the body — Shaw upgraded "delegate" to "delete."
2. **What you'll learn.** Exactly 3 bullets, action verb first, key phrase bolded mid-bullet. Bullet 1 carries the unsignable angle plus proof ("the same approach I've used with **30+ leaders** in 1:1 workshops"); bullets 2–3 name concrete mechanics with the "so that" attached. Name the product features attendees will search for afterward — confirm current product names with Shaw before publishing.
3. **Why this matters.** Three short paragraphs: the enemy (behavioral beats competitive — "most leaders still treat AI like a search bar"), the proof (two quantified wins beat five vague ones; pull from the Testimonials database, keep anonymized wins anonymous), and what the reader leaves with.
4. **Who you'll learn from.** Re-cut the bio per audience — lead with the credential *this* reader cares about (execs: "taught 30+ executives and senior leaders in 1:1 workshops"; engineers: the builder/YouTube credentials). The PhD and 100k+ YouTube audience are supporting cast unless the audience is builders.

## Worked example (Claude for Executives, July 2026)

**Title:** Claude for Executives: How to Delete 20 Hours of Busywork a Month

**What you'll learn**
- Delegate work to Claude **like it's a new hire** — the same approach I've used with **30+ leaders** in 1:1 workshops
- Connect Claude to **the apps where your work actually lives** — email, calendar, docs — with Cowork
- Build **custom skills with a 4-step framework** so recurring tasks (briefings, reports, follow-ups) run themselves

**Why this matters**

You already know how to delegate. But most leaders still treat AI like a search bar: type a question, get an answer, do the real work themselves.

The leaders getting real time back treat Claude like a new hire instead. One built skills that cut a 10-hour weekly newsletter to 1 hour. Another turned vendor assessments that took 1–2 hours each into 10–15 in 40 minutes.

In this session, I'll walk through the exact approach I use in my 1:1 workshops, so you leave knowing which 20 hours of your month to hand off first.

## Locked language

- **"New hire" is the locked delegation metaphor** — it beat "intern" (reads beneath an exec's dignity to manage), "team member" (soft), and "assistant."
- Weight testimonial-language edits by ICP fit before treating them as gospel — a small-startup founder's edit is weaker evidence about midsized-company leaders than a matching edit from a target reader.
