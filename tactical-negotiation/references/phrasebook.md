# Phrasebook

The full tagged phrase library. Grab only the tag you need. Fill blanks (`__`)
with the specifics of the situation, and reshape into the user's voice — these
are patterns, not scripts to paste verbatim.

## LABELS
Format: "It seems like…" / "It sounds like…" / "It feels like…" (never "I think you…").

**Surfacing hidden resistance**
- It seems like something is bothering you.
- It seems like something is holding you back.
- It seems like there's something here that bothers you.
- It feels like there's something in the back of your mind.
- It seems like there's more here than meets the eye.
- It sounds like there's more going on here.
- It feels like you have some concerns / reservations.
- It seems like you're reluctant to __.
- It seems like you are worried that __.

**Naming their values / priorities**
- It seems like __ is valuable to you.
- It seems like you value __.
- It seems like you don't like __.
- It seems like __ makes it easier.
- It seems like you have something in mind. *(instead of "how do you want to proceed?")*
- It seems like you've given this a lot of thought.
- Wow — it seems like you have a really good reason for that.
- It seems like you have a good reason for asking that.
- Seems like you've got a reason for saying that.
- It sounds like this is really important.

**Naming their difficulty / pressure**
- It sounds like you're in a tough situation.
- It sounds like I put you in a difficult situation.
- It seems like you've got a lot on your mind.
- This probably feels like a slap in the face.

**Repairing trust / owning a failure**
- You feel like I've failed you.
- It feels like I haven't 100% earned your trust. *(surfaces the real objection)*
- It sounds like you've really got your guard up.

**Self-directed / positioning**
- It seems my company is in a unique position…
- It seems like I am missing something here.
- It feels like I'm missing the bigger picture.
- It feels like I'm missing something.

**Pitch / value objections**
- It sounds like the value just isn't there for you. *(when asked to keep pitching)*
- Feels like you're bored with this stuff.

**When they're shopping you around**
- Seems like you're still shopping around.
- Seems like you're still in the information-gathering process.
- Seems like you've got somebody else you really want to do business with.

**Salary-specific**
- It sounds like you guys already have a range in mind.

## CALIBRATED QUESTIONS
"How" and "what" questions that hand them the problem. Avoid "why."

**Diagnosing the objection**
- What about this doesn't work for you?
- What about this works for you? / What about this is important to you?
- What makes you say that? / What makes you ask?
- What's the core issue here? / What's the objective — what are we trying to accomplish?
- What is it that brought us into this situation?
- You've said X, but also said Y — how do those things add up?

**Moving toward a solution**
- What would you need to make it work?
- What would you like me to do? / How would you like me to proceed?
- How can I help make this better for us?
- How can we solve this problem?
- What do you think a fair deal looks like here?
- What else could you offer to make that a good offer for me?
- What assurances can you give to make that work for me?
- What's the next step?

**Gentle "no" / pushing back without a "no"**
- How am I supposed to do that? / How am I supposed to make that work?
- How am I supposed to accept that?
- How am I supposed to answer that?
- How is that worthwhile?
- How does this look to you? *(after "how are we supposed to do that if you [threat]?")*

**Stress-testing an idea (let them spot the flaw)**
- What happens if you do nothing?
- When this goes wrong, what are we going to do?
- When we run out of money, what's going to happen?
- How do we keep this on track? How do we know if it's off track? If it gets off track, how do we fix it?

**Surfacing absent stakeholders**
- How does this affect the rest of your team?
- How on board are the people not on this call?
- What do your colleagues see as their main challenge here?

**Big-picture / alignment**
- How does this fit into the objective / bigger picture?
- How does making this deal resonate with what your company prides itself on?
- What are we up against here?
- What do you see as the most difficult thing to get around?
- What's the biggest challenge you've faced?

**Making yourself indispensable / career**
- Why me? How could I help you? What could I do for you?
- How can I be involved in the projects critical to the company's future?
- Why did you pick my application out of dozens of qualified people?

**Envisioning the future together**
- How do we move on from here? *(are you in their future plans?)*
- What do you want to do now?
- On a scale of 1 to 10, what are the chances we get this deal?

## NO-ORIENTED QUESTIONS
Engineer a "no" so they feel safe and in control.

- Are you against __? / Do you disagree with __?
- Would it be ridiculous to __? / Would it be impossible to __?
- Would it be a bad / crazy / bizarre idea to __?
- Is it a bad time to talk?
- Do you think it makes sense for me to __? *(slip in your idea)*
- Do you think it's fair for me to __?
- Have you given up on this? *(silence = yes, they have)*

## SOFTENERS / EMOTIONAL ANCHORS
Pre-load bad news so it lands soft.

- It may not be a bad idea to…
- You're probably not going to like me very much…
- You're probably not going to like me after I say this…
- This may sound crazy… / This may be a ridiculous idea…
- I'm sorry… / I'm afraid…
- "You're not gonna like this." (pause) …then ask.
- I've got a lousy proposition…
- I've got a big request / a big ask. *(when asking for action or deliverables)*

## ACCUSATION AUDIT
Say the worst thing they could be thinking about you — first.

- I probably sound like a pain in the butt / a headache.
- I'm sorry — I probably sound like… *(then shut up)*
- Reminder: anytime you feel a "but" coming, shut up instead.

## THE HARD NO
Decline warmly but firmly. No "but."

- Your offer is very generous — I'm sorry, that doesn't work for me.
- I'm sorry, but I'm afraid I just can't do that.
- I'm sorry, that doesn't work for me.
- I don't see how that would ever work.
- Admiration audit: "I really admire / respect your __ … I'm sorry, that just doesn't work."

## WARMTH, GRATITUDE & RAPPORT
Positive emotion, dopamine, in-group building.

- I really admire that you… / I really respect your…
- I really appreciate how generous you're being with me right now.
- Thank you. You've been very generous with your time.
- I'd just like to say thank you for being so patient with me.
- Thank you for your honesty.
- You've done a great job here.
- I'm glad I was able to have a positive impact.
- That's a good idea. *(to encourage more ideas)*
- What do you love about __? *(cold-opener for a bad mood; rapport in business)*
- I'm a huge fan of what you're doing. I'd love to cut a great deal and be part
  of it. Even if we don't get a deal done here and now, I'm always going to be
  rooting for you — and want nothing more than a great long-term relationship.

## DISARMING / HUMANIZING
Lower the stakes, seem human, buy time.

- I don't understand. *(invite them to teach you)*
- I'm sorry… *(to reopen after conflict)*
- I have the memory of an uneducated goldfish — I'm just going to write a few things down.
- I'm here to help. *(instead of "let me know if I can help")*
- I'm here to listen.
- I'm open to new ideas, suggestions, offers.
- Let me ask you something…
- Will / would you help me understand…?
- If I may offer some advice… / offer a suggestion…
- You're in charge here. *(to an assertive)*

## EMPATHY FOR VENTING
When they're complaining or frustrated.

- It sounds like this is very frustrating for you.
- It sounds like this is driving you crazy because no one ever listens to you.
- It sounds like you've been struggling with this for a long time.

## DYNAMIC SILENCE
Shut up and read the room.

- (Go quiet; watch body language and facial expressions.)
- Label the silence: "Seems like there's some stuff going through your mind right now."

---

## SUMMARIES / CHECKING UNDERSTANDING
Trigger "that's right."

- Just to recap (to check my understanding)…
- Please correct me if I'm wrong… / if I'm missing something…
- I want you to feel like you're being treated fairly at all times — so please
  stop me anytime you feel I'm being unfair, and we'll address it.
