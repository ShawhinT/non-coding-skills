# Playbook: Missing Decision-Makers

Goal: when the person in front of you can't actually say yes, stop "closing" them
and start equipping them to close the room you can't see. Surface the real
approval path and arm your champion.

## Sequence
1. **Label the hidden room.**
   > "It feels like there are a few people not on this call who'll weigh in on
   > this."
2. **Gauge their buy-in (calibrated questions).**
   > "How on board are the people not on this call?"
   > "How does this affect the rest of your team?"
3. **Arm your champion with their colleagues' concerns.**
   > "What do your colleagues see as their main challenge here?"
   Now you can hand them the answer to the objection they'll face internally.
4. **Stress-test forward.**
   > "When this goes to the wider group, what's the part they push back on?"
5. **Set the next step explicitly.**
   > "What's the next step to get them comfortable?"

## Highest-leverage lines
- "How on board are the people not on this call?"
- "What do your colleagues see as their main challenge here?"
- "How does this affect the rest of your team?"
- "What's the next step to bring them along?"

## Traps
- Don't push for a yes the person can't give — you'll get a soft yes that dies
  offline.
- Don't skip arming the champion; they'll defend your deal without you in the
  room.
- Don't assume the loudest voice on the call is the real decision-maker.
