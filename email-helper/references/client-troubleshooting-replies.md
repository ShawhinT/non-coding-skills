# Client Troubleshooting and How-Do-I Replies (email register)

A client or workshop participant asks a technical question, reports that something isn't working,
or wants tips on using Claude better. The *content* of the answer, including which shape it takes
(teach, triage, or frame it and take it live), what goes in it, and the look-up of past answers in
the Q&A database, is owned by the `client-support` skill. Invoke it before drafting; this file only
carries what's specific to sending that answer as an email.

**Register.** A teach reply runs 6-10 lines: ack, frame, 3-5 list items, close. Numbered list when
the steps are a sequence; bullets when the items are parallel, each opening with a bold category
label (*Client emails*, *HTML to PPTX*). Skill names bold, lowercase kebab-case. Greeting rules per
SKILL.md (mid-thread, none). Sign "-Shaw" mid-thread, "Cheers, Shaw" on a fresh thread.

**Closer.** The closer carries the email's one `:)` and is a loop, not a CTA: "Let me know how it
goes :)", "Hope that helps :)". For a client inside an engagement with async access, the open door
("Keep me posted and feel free to reach out with any questions any time :)"). When the answer is a
call offer, the Calendly link and its escape hatch per SKILL.md.

**P.S.** A resource link goes in a P.S. per SKILL.md's two-line budget after a list, and so does an
answer riding on an email about something else (a control a leader asked about, a tip from the
last call).

The real sends behind each shape live in `client-support`'s `references/anchors.md`.
