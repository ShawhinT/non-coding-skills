# Weekly Fractional AI Department Syncs

A 30-min standing call with the client's leadership (champion, buyer, ops/people lead), run
off an agenda Shaw writes on the call page: Updates (old business) / Coming up (new business)
/ Blockers. He owns the agenda; prep means gathering what might earn a line and leaving it
under Call Notes for him to promote.

## The test

An item earns a line when it needs the people on the call — their knowledge, their decision,
or their action. Everything else Shaw handles himself, or raises only if someone asks.

## Signal

- **Asks with an owner.** Lead with the owner's name (`[Person]: [Person 2] back from leave?`); the
  name turns a line into a prompt. An update that carries an ask can also sit under Blockers — the repeat is deliberate.
- **Questions only they can answer** — how their internal initiative went, who someone in
  their org is, where a decision landed.
- **Synthesis at leadership altitude.** Patterns across people and process-level opportunities,
  not the individual wins that produced them. Leadership thinks in processes and functions;
  the anecdotes stay in reserve for when someone asks.
- **Stakeholder friction.** A concern from someone in their org that shapes how Shaw can
  operate there (conflicting training messages, overlapping initiatives). Only leadership can
  resolve it, so it surfaces here.
- **Open questions over finished answers.** "Ideas?" invites them to co-own the choice;
  pre-filling it — names, specific asks, a proposed plan — closes the conversation Shaw wants
  them in.

## Noise

- **Resolved elsewhere** (email, Slack) — it already got its airtime.
- **Happening before the sync** — it'll be current by the call; nothing to pre-write.
- **ABA's own bookkeeping** — deliverable counts, build tracking, measurement mechanics. The
  scoreboard is Shaw's to know, not to present, until the term's end is closer than the
  deliverables left; then remaining sessions, unused builds and re-up are the agenda.
- **Detail below leadership altitude** — individual wins, per-seat metrics, tool mechanics.
- **Elaborating standing items** — "Engagement metrics" is one line; Shaw presents it live.

## The sweep

Shaw's call-page notes are reminders; the substance is in transcripts (Meet transcripts are
linked on the pages; Notion meeting notes need `include_transcript` — their AI summaries
misattribute speakers, per `notion-helper`). Gather, since the last sync:

- **Calls and trainings held** with the client (calendar + Notion by domain), one line each
  from the transcript on what it added.
- **Open asks by owner** — sent mail to the client with no reply, and promises made on calls
  with no send behind them. Split what's waiting on them from what's waiting on Shaw.
- **What's booked ahead** through end of term — calls and trainings beyond the standing sync
  itself. Nothing booked is the finding.
- **The client Slack channel** — exports, warnings, and asks land there and nowhere else.

Run it through the test above and write what survives under `## Call Notes` on the sync
page, above anything already there. Leave the agenda alone; Shaw promotes and refines.
