## Who Shaw sells to (the avatar)

AI Builder Academy sells AI enablement / transformation to **50–200-person, English-operating, PE/VC-backed, financial-services-adjacent tech companies** (cyber, fintech, SaaS, investment firms). The buyer is a **two-person committee**:

- **Sponsor** — CEO / founder / Chairman, or CFO / COO. Triggers on **status/FOMO** ("we're behind," "not maximizing it"), closes fast, often *not* hands-on, delegates execution. This is who reach posts are for.
- **Champion / rollout lead** — the person tasked with the rollout (ops or transformation lead, MD, function head). Cares about **how**: governance, adoption, proof of impact. Controls how far the engagement expands. This is who authority posts are for, and who the AI Rollout Audit CTA targets.

**Governance and data security are the universal gate** on every enterprise deal — and the most underused content territory. The free **AI Rollout Audit** is the standing CTA; frame it at the champion but write it to calm the sponsor's "what if our data leaks" fear.

## The barbell content model

Put weight on two ends, starve the mediocre middle.

- **Sponsor posts (reach end):** status, FOMO, strategic risk. e.g. "the gap between people who maximize AI and those who don't," "most founders are barely scratching the surface," business-model disruption takes.
- **Champion posts (authority end):** governance ("deploy without leaking data"), the skill-building method, **quantified outcomes** ("10 hrs/wk → 1").
- **Reach satellite (~1/week):** high-variance, ICP-flavored curiosity or emotion. Broad *within the buyer's world*, never a generic meme that pulls students and other creators.
- **Kill the middle:** abstract analogies aimed at no one (e.g. "rails vs streets"). They get neither reach nor engagement.

## What the data says (recurring patterns)

- **Reach and engagement rate are nearly decoupled.** The widest-reach post is often the lowest-rate. So a reach play doesn't cost engagement quality — but pick *qualified* reach (ICP emotion/status), not random virality.
- **The best single archetype is broad-hook + real substance** — it wins on reach *and* rate. Make it the heaviest slice.
- **Comments are the #1 lever** and the most underused. End posts with one low-friction question; the data shows comment-driving posts pull far ahead.
- **Losers:** feature listicles, abstract analogies, off-thesis topics, generic memes.

## Cadence and sequencing (~5 posts/week)

- Rough mix: ~2 sponsor / ~2 champion / ~1 reach satellite.
- **Front-load reach** (Mon–Wed) so early engagers prime the algorithm to surface later posts to them; put authority and any hard CTA later in the week.
- **Soft-CTA** the AI Rollout Audit inside high-performing value posts; run a **hard CTA every 2–4 weeks**, timed to a week that's already hot.
- For event promos, **double up** on a day rather than displacing a CTA, and keep the two asks on separate days.

## The "what separates winners" lens (for the brainstorm gap-check)

When generating and pruning, contrast against what's worked: belief/POV + substance and status/FOMO openers win; abstract analogies, feature roundups, and off-thesis posts sink. Every proposed week should have at least one governance post and one quantified-proof post, and every post should have a closing-question idea.
