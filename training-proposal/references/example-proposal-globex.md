## Engagement Goal

By the end of the engagement:

1. The whole org leaves with a shared mental model of AI, LLMs, and Agents.
2. Each team is onboarded and activated on their respective tool (i.e. Claude Code for Engineering, Claude Cowork for everyone else).
3. Teams can turn any recurring workflow into a Claude skill using the 4-step skill-building framework.

---

## Sessions

### Intake Form *(submitted 7 days before Kickoff)*

A single intake form goes out to all participants ahead of the kickoff. It gauges baseline AI familiarity and surfaces real workflows for breakout sessions.

### All-Hands Kickoff (30 min)

- **Introduction** *(5 min)*
    - About the instructor(s)
    - Training agenda

- **3 Types of AI** *(5 min)*
    - Software 1.0: Rule-based systems
    - Software 2.0: Machine Learning
    - Software 3.0: Agentic AI

- **Software 3.0 Deep Dive** *(15 min)*
    - What's an LLM?
    - What's an AI Agent?
    - Connectors & Skills
    - Context management

- **Wrap-Up & What's Next** *(5 min)*

- **Optional Q&A** *(15 min, after the session)*

### Cohort Breakouts (90 min each)

- **Level Set & Tool Deep Dive** *(15 min)*
    - Recap of key concepts from the kickoff
    - Tool Overview: Claude Code (Engineering) or Claude Cowork (Product / Growth / Operations)

- **4-Step Framework for Building Skills** *(5 min)*
    - Do it once → distill → test → improve

- **Live Build: 3 Skills** *(60 min)*
    - Three skills built live against use cases pre-selected from the intake form, with Q&A and troubleshooting throughout

- **Homework & Wrap-Up** *(10 min)*
    - Each participant builds one skill before office hours


### Cohort Office Hours (30 min each, ~1 week later)

- Volunteers share what they built for homework
- Live troubleshooting and follow-up questions
- Instructor shares roadmap for 2-3 skills the team can build next

---

### Additional Deliverables

- 30-minute debrief with [Stakeholder] and the AI transformation team after all sessions are complete
- A one-page rollout memo summarizing what worked, what didn't, and where to expand next

## Logistics

- **Prerequisites:** All participants need an active Claude account (Team or Enterprise). Engineering should have Claude Code installed. Product, Growth, and Operations should have the desktop app installed.
- **Scheduling:** The 4 cohort breakouts run across 1-2 days depending on team availability. Each cohort's office hour is scheduled ~1 week after its breakout.
- **Intake form:** A single form goes out to all participants before the kickoff (see above). Responses are used to pre-select the three use cases for each cohort's breakout so they must be filled out **no later than 7 days prior** to training sessions.
- **Communication channel (optional):** A shared channel in the team's internal tool (Slack, Teams, etc.) is stood up before the kickoff so participants can share skills, ask questions, and keep momentum between sessions.
