# Topics (picking what the video is about)

The topic decides how much the title and hook can do. A topic with built-in pull hands the title its click-drivers; a topic with none forces the title to fake them, which reads as clickbait. So pick the topic for attention before writing a single word of the package.

## Where topics come from

Shaw's edge is that he's the only one who has lived his experience, so the strongest topics are things only he can say. Three wells, roughly in order of how often they pay off:

- **Recent past: what happened this month.** Calls, workshops, client wins, a question three prospects asked. This is the deepest well and is already mined (ABA Calls, convo-mining databases, intake and testimonial forms). Client results told so they teach the viewer something promote the offer and give value at once.
- **Manufactured: do something in order to report on it.** Run an experiment, set a constraint, build the thing, then film the result. First-person experiments with concrete numbers are what the channel's outliers share (see `titles.md` → Step 0), and an experiment is the cleanest way to own a real number.
- **Far past: an older lesson Shaw lived.** PhD research, building credit-risk models at Toyota, running IT at a car dealership, early SaaS builds. Tell the lesson, not the backstory ("the story without the scar"), and connect it to a problem the avatar has today. If the link to the avatar's world can't be stated in one sentence, the story is a memoir, not a topic.

The channel doesn't do news. Trending topics and "X just launched" angles are out — they decay, and the titles are built to stay searchable for months.

## The pull check

Before committing, name which of these the topic carries on its own. Aim for at least two:

- **Relevancy** — it's about the avatar's specific world (their team, their tools, their board), not people in general.
- **Conflict** — two things in tension: a belief vs. the evidence, the common approach vs. what works, Shaw vs. conventional wisdom.
- **Unusual** — something outside the norm: an odd result, an extreme number, a setup nobody else would try.
- **Authority** — a recognizable name attached: Anthropic, a known company, a real client result.
- **Ongoing** — part of a story still unfolding (a series, an experiment with more episodes, a build in progress), so viewers come back for what happens next.

A topic that scores zero or one is a weak topic, not a title problem. Change the angle (find the conflict, pick the unusual case, run the experiment) before moving on to titles.

## Output

For each candidate topic: one sentence on the topic, its source well, the pulls it carries, and the one-line link to the avatar's pain. Recommend one, then hand off to `titles.md`.
