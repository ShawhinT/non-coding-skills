# Pricing

Kept out of SKILL.md on purpose: the skill loader treats a dollar sign followed by a digit as an argument slot, so prices inline in SKILL.md arrive garbled whenever the skill is invoked with arguments.

## Hourly rates

- **$2,500/hr** for prepared content (lectures, webinars, custom sessions)
- **$1,000/hr** for no-prep (workshops, office hours)

For custom sessions, price = `duration_hours × hourly_rate`. Ask which tier applies to each session.

## Standard session rates

Recurring formats have go-to flat rates (round numbers, not strict `duration × rate`). Short and done-for-you sessions are priced as packaged minimums.

| Session type | Standard rate |
|---|---|
| 1:1 Claude Workshop (90-min session + 30-min check-in) | $2,250 single, $1,500 each at 3+ |
| Team workshop (60-90 min, any group size) | $5,000 |
| All-hands kickoff (short, prepared) | $2,500 flat (often priced as a $5,000 team workshop in a proposal set) |
| Office hour / follow-up (~30 min, no-prep) | $1,000 |

The 3+ rate is shown as a list price plus a green "Volume discount (3+ 1:1 workshops)" row, not as a lower unit price.

## Fractional AI Department (3-month retainer)

Founding client rate steps up per client: $6k → $7.5k → $9k → $12k → $15k/mo. Show the list price ($12,000/mo, $36,000 for the quarter) with a green founding client discount row down to the current rung. Re-price at whatever rung is current; an old quote doesn't hold.

### Additional Services (in-engagement rates)

Listed last in a fractional proposal, after Guarantees and Payment terms.

| Service | Standard | Client rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1:1 workshop (90 min + 30 min check-in) | $2,250 | $1,500 |
| Team workshop (60-90 min) | $5,000 | $3,750 |
| Custom AI build (done-for-you workflow build) | Quoted per project | 25% off |

These are starting points to confirm with the user, not fixed outputs.
