AI Use Cases for Restaurants

For consultants

01

Contract and spec review

Compare the equipment spec you sent with what came back. Claude flags missed or swapped items before they cost the project.

02

Equipment quote builder

Drop in a vendor quote. Claude pulls the models and SKUs into an equipment schedule and a client-ready quote.

03

Job prospecting

Claude finds new design projects, tracks your emails, and drafts the follow-ups. Business development without a hire.

For operators

04

Menu and pricing optimization

Claude reviews menu mix, prices, and drink margins against your costs. A first-time owner gets years of operator know-how on call.

05

Social media marketer

Upload a food photo and get an on-brand Instagram post. A shop with no marketer still shows up locally.

06

Promo flyer design

Flyers for specials, events, and limited-time offers, on brand and done in minutes. No designer needed.

07

Review response drafting

Claude drafts replies to Google reviews, especially the negative ones that wear owners down.

08

Catering sales outreach

Claude researches nearby offices and venues and sends tailored catering pitches. Catering becomes a pipeline, not word of mouth.

09

Photo-to-SOP manual

Photograph each station step and get an illustrated SOP back. One operator went from 4 to 5 hours per manual to about 15 minutes.

10

Multi-unit P&L summary

Monthly exports from every location become one page: sales, labor %, food cost %, and the outliers worth a call.

11

Month-end close

Claude cleans up each store's accounting exports and flags errors and unrecorded transfers. Close goes from days to hours.

12

Industry news digest

A daily brief on restaurant industry and competitor news. One operator saves about 2.5 hours a week.

13

Hourly hiring pipeline

Claude screens crew applicants against a rubric and turns interview notes into scorecards. Built for kitchens that hire every week.
